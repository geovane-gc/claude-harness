# How request

## Code
Each repo to read, its path and the commit to read it at.

## Goal
The question, or the change it prepares, in one or two sentences: the change itself, not a flow to
explain.

## Already known
What you have read or measured, one line each with its `file:line` or its source.

## Reader
Who reads the explanation next and the one decision it feeds, in one line, such as "the architect,
choosing the shape of X". The coordinator works out what that decision needs.

## Out of scope
What the explanation leaves out.

## Output
`<run folder>/how/`: the coordinator writes `explanation.md` there, and the explorers their findings.
